title: "Engagement workspaces for shared operator work"
---

Napad engagements now run inside a shared workspace. Operators collect evidence in one place, replay attack chains step by step, and see each other's actions in real time so a team can pick up where another member left off. Workspace state is recorded against the engagement record for later review and reporting.
