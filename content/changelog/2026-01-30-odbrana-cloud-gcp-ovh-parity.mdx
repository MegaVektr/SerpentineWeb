Cloud Scan now applies the same misconfiguration ruleset, attack-path analysis, and compliance benchmarks to GCP and OVH that it already applies to AWS. Cross-account and cross-project relationships are captured so an attack path can traverse providers in a single graph. Findings continue to map to the canonical model shared with Napad and Regulativa.
