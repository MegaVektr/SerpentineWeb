title: "Cross-product evidence linking is GA"
---

Evidence records now reference findings across products. A Postava hardening run can cite the Odbrana audit finding it remediates, and a Napad validation attaches to the same record once it confirms the issue is closed. The canonical model carries the link end-to-end, so reports stay consistent regardless of which product surfaced the evidence first.
