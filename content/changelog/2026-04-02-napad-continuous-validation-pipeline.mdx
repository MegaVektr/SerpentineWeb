title: "Continuous validation from Odbrana findings"
---

Napad now accepts Odbrana findings as validation inputs through a scope-bound queue. Teams define rules that promote specific finding classes — exposed cloud assets, exploitable web issues, supply-chain risks — into the next validation engagement, so confirmation work tracks discovery without manual handoff. Scope rules respect the engagement boundaries already configured in the workspace.
