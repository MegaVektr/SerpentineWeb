title: "Blockchain Scan adds Rust and Solana coverage"
---

Odbrana's blockchain scanner now analyzes Rust-based Solana programs alongside Solidity contracts. Coverage includes account validation, signer verification, and Solana-specific patterns such as missing ownership checks and program-derived address misuse. Findings flow into the canonical model with the same evidence shape as the existing Solidity rules.
